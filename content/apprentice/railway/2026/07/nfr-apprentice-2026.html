RRC NFR Apprentices Recruitment 2026 – Post Details
Post Name Total Posts Educational Qualification (Eligibility)
RRC NFR Apprentices 6777 Candidates must have passed Class 10 (Matriculation) with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (the minimum marks requirement is not applicable for SC/ST/PwBD candidates) and possess a valid NCVT/SCVT ITI Certificate in the relevant trade.

For Medical Laboratory Technician (Pathology/Radiology): Candidates must have passed 10th and 12th (Science) with Physics, Chemistry & Biology (PCB), securing at least 50% aggregate marks in both examinations.
