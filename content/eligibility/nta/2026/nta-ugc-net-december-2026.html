Post Name Eligibility
Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) Master's degree or equivalent with 55% marks for General/Unreserved/General-EWS candidates and 50% marks for OBC-NCL/SC/ST/PwD/PwBD/Third Gender candidates. Final-year/result-awaited candidates may apply provisionally, subject to prescribed conditions.
Assistant Professor Master's degree or equivalent with 55% marks for General/Unreserved/General-EWS candidates and 50% marks for OBC-NCL/SC/ST/PwD/PwBD/Third Gender candidates. Final-year/result-awaited candidates may apply provisionally.
Ph.D. Admission Candidates qualifying through UGC-NET can be eligible for Ph.D. admission as per applicable UGC-NET provisions. Candidates qualifying through a Four-Year Bachelor's Degree can be eligible for Ph.D. admission, but not for appointment as Assistant Professor.
