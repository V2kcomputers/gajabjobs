Paper Teaching Level Educational Qualification
Paper 1 Primary Level
(Class 1 to 5)
  • Graduation with 2-year BTC / Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed.).
  • Graduation with D.El.Ed. (Appearing candidates).
  • Class 12th with at least 50% marks and 4-year Bachelor of Elementary Education (B.El.Ed.).
Paper 2 Upper Primary Level
(Class 6 to 8)
  • Graduation with 2-year BTC / Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed.).
  • Graduation with B.Ed. or D.El.Ed. (Appearing candidates).
  • Class 12th with at least 50% marks and 4-year Bachelor of Elementary Education (B.El.Ed.).
  • Class 12th with at least 50% marks and 4-year B.A.-B.Ed. / B.Sc.-B.Ed.
Note: Candidates eligible for both levels can apply for Paper 1 and Paper 2.

*For detailed eligibility criteria, please read the official notification carefully.
