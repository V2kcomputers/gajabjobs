ISRO Recruitment 2026 Post Wise Vacancy & Educational Qualification
Post Name Total Posts Educational Qualification
Assistant / Upper Division Clerk (UDC) 150 Graduation with a minimum of 60% marks or CGPA 6.32/10 from a recognized University. The degree must have been completed within the prescribed duration of the course. Candidates should also have proficiency in the use of Computers.
Junior Personal Assistant (JPA) / Stenographer 92 Graduation with a minimum of 60% marks or CGPA 6.32/10 from a recognized University OR Diploma in Commercial/Secretarial Practice with at least 60% marks or CGPA 6.32/10, along with one year of experience as a Steno-Typist/Stenographer. Candidates must also possess English Stenography Speed of 60 WPM and proficiency in the use of Computers.
Note: Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully for detailed eligibility criteria, experience requirements, stenography skill, and other conditions before applying.
