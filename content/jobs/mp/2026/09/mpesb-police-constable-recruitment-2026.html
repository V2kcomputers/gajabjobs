MP Police Constable Recruitment 2026 Short Details
Post Name Total Posts Educational Qualification
Constable (GD) – Special Armed Force (SAF) 700 General/OBC/SC candidates must have passed Class 10th or Higher Secondary/equivalent examination, while ST candidates must have passed Class 8th or equivalent examination.
Constable (GD) – Other than Special Armed Force (DEF) 6,800 General/OBC/SC candidates must have passed Class 10th or Higher Secondary/equivalent examination, while ST candidates must have passed Class 8th or equivalent examination.
Total 7,500 Posts
Note: Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully for detailed eligibility criteria and other important requirements.
Physical Efficiency Test (PET)
Test Physical Efficiency Test will be conducted and evaluated under the supervision of the Selection Committee constituted by the Director General of Police.
Selection for PET Candidates up to seven times the advertised vacancies against each vertical category will be shortlisted for the next stage based on the cut-off percentile of the First Stage Written Examination. All candidates securing the cut-off percentile will be called for PET, even if the number exceeds seven times the advertised vacancies.
Minimum Qualifying Marks Candidates must secure at least 30% marks (30 out of 100) in the Physical Efficiency Test. PET marks will be added to the First Stage Written Examination marks for preparation of the final merit list.
PET Marks Marks will be awarded as per Schedule-I of the recruitment rules.
