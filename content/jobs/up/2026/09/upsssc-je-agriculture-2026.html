Post Name Total Posts Eligibility / Educational Qualification
Junior Engineer (Agriculture) 134
  • Candidate must have passed High School examination from the Board of High School and Intermediate Education, Uttar Pradesh, or an equivalent examination recognized by the Government.
  • Candidate must possess a 3-Year Diploma in Agricultural Engineering from the Uttar Pradesh Board of Technical Education or an equivalent qualification recognized by the Government.
  • The prescribed equivalent technical qualification must be approved/recognized by AICTE, wherever applicable.
  • Candidate must have appeared in UPSSSC PET-2025.
  • Candidate must be eligible/shortlisted by UPSSSC for appearing in the Junior Engineer (Agriculture) Main Examination on the basis of PET-2025.
Important Note: Candidates are advised to carefully read the official notification for detailed eligibility, educational qualification, age limit, reservation, and other eligibility conditions before applying.
