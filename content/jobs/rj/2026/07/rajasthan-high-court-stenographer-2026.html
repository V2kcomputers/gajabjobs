Post Name Total Posts Educational Qualification (Eligibility)
Stenographer Grade-III (Hindi) 141 Candidates must have passed Senior Secondary (10+2) or its equivalent from a recognized Board.

Must possess a good working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari Script and knowledge of Rajasthani Dialects.

Candidates must possess any one of the following Computer Qualifications:
  • DOEACC / NIELIT 'O' Level Certificate
  • Computer Operator & Programming Assistant (COPA) / DPCS Certificate
  • Diploma in Computer Science / Computer Application
  • Diploma in Computer Science & Engineering
  • Rajasthan State Certificate Course in Information Technology (RSCIT)
  • Senior Secondary with Computer Science
  • Any equivalent or higher computer qualification recognized by the Government.
Note: For detailed eligibility criteria and accepted equivalent qualifications, candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully.
