  • OR MCA / M.Tech / M.Sc. in relevant Computer Science, IT, Software Engineering, Electronics or related disciplines with minimum 50% marks.
  • Certification: Must hold one or more valid prescribed certifications such as OSCP, OSCE³, OSWE, OSEP, GIAC GPEN/GXPN, CAISP, SANS Offensive AI or SANS GenAI & LLM Security.
  • Experience: Minimum 3 years post-qualification hands-on experience in offensive cyber security.
  • Experience may include Red Team Operations, AI Red Team Operations, Penetration Testing, Vulnerability Assessment/Exploitation or Cyber Security Testing of AI/LLM systems.
  • Preferred: Advanced degree in Cyber Security or Information Technology.
