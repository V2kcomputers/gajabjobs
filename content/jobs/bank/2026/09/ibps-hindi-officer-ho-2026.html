IBPS Hindi Officer Recruitment 2026 Short Details
Post Name Eligibility
Hindi Officer (Grade E)
  • Master’s Degree in Hindi with English as a major or elective subject at Graduation.
  • OR Master’s Degree in English with Hindi as a major or elective subject at Graduation.
  • OR Master’s Degree in any subject other than Hindi or English, with Hindi as a major or elective subject and English as the medium of examination at degree level.
  • OR Master’s Degree in any subject other than Hindi or English, with English as a major or elective subject and Hindi as the medium of examination at degree level.
  • One year translation experience from English to Hindi and Hindi to English in a bank/financial institution is desirable.
  • Computer proficiency is mandatory, especially in MS Word and MS Excel in Hindi and English.
  • Exposure or experience in developing AI-based translation tools will be an added qualification.

Note: Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully for detailed eligibility criteria.
