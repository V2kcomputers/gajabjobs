Indian Army JAG Entry Scheme 125th Course (April 2027) – Vacancy Details
Post Name Total Posts Educational Qualification (Eligibility)
Short Service Commission (JAG) – 125th Course (April 2027) 10 Candidates must possess an LLB Degree (3-year or 5-year course) with a minimum of 55% aggregate marks from a Bar Council of India (BCI) recognized university. Applicants must have appeared in the CLAT PG 2026 examination and should be eligible for registration as an Advocate with the Bar Council of India/State.
Total 10 Posts
Note: Both unmarried male and unmarried female Law Graduates are eligible to apply. Candidates should read the official notification carefully before submitting the online application.
