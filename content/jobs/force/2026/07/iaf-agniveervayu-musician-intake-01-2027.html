Indian Air Force Agniveervayu (Musician) Recruitment 01/2027 – Post Details
Post Name Educational Qualification / Eligibility
Agniveervayu (Musician) Educational Qualification: Passed Matriculation (10th) or equivalent with minimum pass marks from any Central, State, or UT Government recognized Board/School.

Music Eligibility:
  • Must have proficiency in music with correct tempo, pitch, rhythm, and singing ability.
  • Should be able to perform one complete song and play at least one musical instrument from the prescribed list.
  • Must possess any one of the following:
    • Grade 5 or above from a recognized music institute (e.g. Trinity College London, ABRSM, KM Music Conservatory, Bangalore School of Music, Berklee, etc.).
    • Diploma or equivalent in Hindustani or Carnatic Classical Music from a recognized University.
    • Certificate of participation or awards in recognized music events (where applicable).
Note: Please read the official notification carefully for detailed eligibility criteria.
