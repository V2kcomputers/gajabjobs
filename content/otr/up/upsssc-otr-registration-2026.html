UPSSSC One Time Registration (OTR) 2026 – Registration & Minimum Eligibility Details
Post / Registration Minimum Eligibility
UPSSSC One Time Registration (OTR) Any candidate intending to apply for future UPSSSC recruitments can complete One Time Registration (OTR). A valid mobile number, email ID, recent passport-size photograph, and required personal details are mandatory.
Note: OTR does not specify any educational qualification. The eligibility criteria (education, age limit, experience, etc.) will be mentioned separately in the official notification of each UPSSSC recruitment.
