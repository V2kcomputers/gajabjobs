Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Class XI Admission 2027 Short Details
Post Name Eligibility
JNV Class XI Lateral Entry Admission 2027-28
  • Candidate must be studying in Class 10 during the academic session 2026-27.
  • Candidate must be studying in a Government/Government-recognized school.
  • The school must be located in the same district where the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya (JNV) is located.
  • Candidate must be an Indian National studying Class 10 in India.
  • Date of Birth must be between 01 June 2010 and 31 July 2012, both dates inclusive.
  • Candidates who have passed Class 10 before the 2026-27 session are not eligible.
  • For district-level merit, the candidate's residence and Class 10 study district must be the same.
  • Admission is only against vacant seats likely to be available in JNVs for the 2027-28 session.

Note: For detailed eligibility and other admission conditions, candidates should read the official notification/prospectus carefully.
