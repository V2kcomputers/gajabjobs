Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Class 9th Admission 2027 Short Details
Post Name Eligibility
JNV Class IX Lateral Entry Admission 2027-28
  • Candidate must be a bona fide resident of the concerned district where admission is sought.
  • Must be studying Class VIII during Academic Session 2026-27 in a Government/Government-recognized school of the same district where the JNV is located.
  • Date of Birth must be between 01 May 2012 and 31 July 2014 (both dates inclusive).
  • Only Indian Nationals studying Class VIII in India are eligible.
  • Candidates who have already passed Class VIII in an earlier academic session are not eligible.

Note: For detailed eligibility criteria, candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully.
