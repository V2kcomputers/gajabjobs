BPSSC Company Commander HG Online Form 2026
BPSSC has released Advertisement No. 11/2026 for 65 Company Commander posts in the Bihar Home Guard Service Cadre. Eligible candidates must be trained Home Guards with at least 5 years of service/duty and hold a graduation degree from a recognized university or equivalent qualification. The age limit is 24 to 50 years as on 01/08/2026. Selection includes the Preliminary Written Examination, Main Written Examination and Physical Efficiency Test. Online applications are scheduled from 10/09/2026 to 10/10/2026, with an application fee of ₹100.

BOI Specialist Officer SO Online Form 2026
Bank of India (BOI) has released the SO Recruitment 2026 notification under Project No. 2026-27/02 for recruitment of Officers in various specialist streams from Scale-I to Scale-IV. A total of 205 vacancies are available. Online applications will be accepted from 10/09/2026 to 25/09/2026. The age and qualification cut-off date is 01/08/2026. Candidates must fulfil the post-wise educational qualification and experience requirements. Selection will be through Online Examination and/or Personal Interview, depending on the number of applicants. The application fee is ₹1,180 for General/EWS/OBC candidates and ₹175 for SC/ST/PwBD candidates.
