RPSC Physiotherapist Online Form 2026
The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has invited online applications for the recruitment of 20 Physiotherapist (NSA) posts under the Medical and Health Department, Government of Rajasthan. The online application window will remain open from 23 July 2026 to 21 August 2026. Candidates who have completed Senior Secondary (10+2) along with a Diploma in Physiotherapy from a recognized institution are eligible to apply. Applicants should also possess working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari Script and knowledge of Rajasthan Culture. The prescribed age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2027, with age relaxation available for reserved categories as per Rajasthan Government rules. Interested candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully before submitting the online application.

MP Anganwadi Bharti 2026 Provisional List (अनंतिम सूची) Released
The Women and Child Development Department, Madhya Pradesh has invited online applications for MP Anganwadi Recruitment 2026 to fill 2,548 vacancies, including 781 Anganwadi Worker and 1,767 Anganwadi Assistant posts. Female candidates who have passed 12th and belong to the same Gram Panchayat (rural) or the same Ward (urban) where the vacancy is available are eligible to apply. The online application process starts on 01 July 2026 and closes on 13 July 2026, while the correction facility will remain available until 15 July 2026. Applicants must be between 18 and 35 years of age as on 01 January 2026 and are required to pay an application fee of ₹100 plus 18% GST through the MP Online Selection Portal.

IGCAR Trade Apprentice Online Form 2026
The Indira Gandhi Centre for Atomic Research (IGCAR), Kalpakkam, has invited online applications for 200 Trade Apprentice posts under the Apprentices Act, 1961. Candidates holding an ITI certificate in the relevant trade can apply online from 20/07/2026 to 19/08/2026. The apprenticeship duration is one year, with a monthly stipend of ₹11,040 for 2-year ITI holders and ₹10,560 for 1-year ITI holders. Selection will be based on ITI merit, followed by document verification and a medical examination. Interested candidates should carefully read the official notification before submitting the online application.
