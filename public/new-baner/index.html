BPCL Non-Management Online Form 2026
Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited (BPCL) has invited online applications for 154 Non-Management posts at its Mumbai and Kochi Refineries. Vacancies are available for Process Technician (Chemical), Process Operator (Chemical), Technician/Operator (Mechanical), and Technician (Electrical). Candidates holding a full-time Diploma in the relevant engineering discipline with at least one year of post-qualification experience or apprenticeship training can apply. The online application window is open from 29/07/2026 to 13/08/2026. The selection process includes a Computer Based Test (CBT), Skill/Proficiency Test (SPT), Document Verification, and Medical Examination. Selected candidates will receive a pay scale of ₹26,500–₹34,000 along with applicable BPCL allowances and benefits.

ISRO Scientist / Engineer Online Form 2026
The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) has invited online applications for 92 Scientist/Engineer 'SC' vacancies in various engineering disciplines. Eligible candidates with a BE/B.Tech degree (minimum 65% marks or 6.84 CGPA) and a valid GATE 2025 or GATE 2026 score can apply online from 28 July 2026 to 17 August 2026. Applicants should verify their eligibility and submit the online application before the closing date.

Rajasthan Safai Karamchari Bharti Online Form 2026
The Local Self Government Department, Government of Rajasthan has invited online applications for Rajasthan Contractual Safai Karamchari Recruitment 2026 to fill 24,752 vacancies in Urban Local Bodies (ULBs). The online application process will be conducted from 15 August 2026 to 28 September 2026. Candidates must be residents of Rajasthan and have at least one year of sanitation work experience in a Rajasthan ULB, while no minimum educational qualification has been prescribed. Selection will be made through a lottery system followed by document verification. Applicants should carefully review the official notification before submitting the online application.
