BPSSC Police Sub Inspector Online Form 2026
The Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) has invited online applications for the recruitment of 150 Police Sub Inspector (General Closed Cadre) posts under Advertisement No. 09/2026. HSSC CET Group D Online Form 2026
Haryana Staff Selection Commission (HSSC) has invited online applications for CET Group D Recruitment 2026. Candidates who have passed Matriculation (10th Class) with Hindi or Sanskrit as a subject are eligible to apply. The online application process begins on 19/06/2026 and will remain open until 03/07/2026. The age limit is 18 to 42 years with relaxation available for reserved categories as per Haryana Government rules. Selection will be based on the Common Eligibility Test (CET) followed by document verification. Interested candidates should read the official notification carefully and complete the online application before the last date.

RSSB CET Graduate Level Online Form 2026
RSSB has officially released the Rajasthan CET Graduate Level 2026 Notification for candidates seeking graduate-level government jobs in Rajasthan. The online application process will begin on 04 July 2026 and continue until 02 August 2026 through the SSO Portal. Candidates with a Bachelor's Degree from a recognized university are eligible to apply. The CET Graduate Level examination will be held from 01 December to 03 December 2026 and serves as the eligibility test for various posts, including Patwari, Village Development Officer (VDO), Junior Accountant, Platoon Commander, Deputy Jailor, Hostel Superintendent Grade-II, Supervisor, and other graduate-level recruitments notified by RSSB.
