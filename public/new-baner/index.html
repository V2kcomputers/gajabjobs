BPSSC Forest Range Officer (FRO) Online Form 2026
BPSSC has invited online applications for the Forest Range Officer (FRO) Recruitment 2026 under the Environment, Forest & Climate Change Department, Bihar. A total of 16 vacancies are available for eligible Indian candidates. Applicants with a Bachelor's Degree in Agriculture, Forestry, Botany, Zoology, Veterinary Science, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, Statistics, Geology, Engineering, or equivalent disciplines can apply online from 16/07/2026 to 16/08/2026. The recruitment process includes a Written Examination, Interview, Physical Standard Test (PST), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), and Medical Examination. Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully before applying to ensure they meet all eligibility and physical requirements.
