UPPSC Architectural Assistant, Estate Manager, Microbiologist 2026
The Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) has invited online applications for the Recruitment 2026 to fill 37 vacancies for the posts of Architectural Assistant, Estate Manager, and Microbiologist. Interested candidates should carefully check the post-wise eligibility, educational qualifications, and experience requirements mentioned in the official notification before applying online.

SBI Circle Based Officer CBO Marks 2026
State Bank of India has released the SBI CBO Recruitment 2026 notification for 2050 Circle Based Officer posts. Online applications are invited from 29 January to 18 February 2026.
