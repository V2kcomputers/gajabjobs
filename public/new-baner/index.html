UPSSSC Technical Assistant Group C Admit Card 2026
The UPSSSC Technical Assistant Recruitment 2026 invites applications for 2759 vacancies. Applicants must possess a Bachelor's Degree in Agriculture or related field along with a valid PET 2025 score. The online application process begins on 22 May 2026 and closes on 11 June 2026. The application fee is ₹25 for all categories. Selection will be done through PET-based shortlisting, written examination, and document verification. Both male and female candidates are eligible.
