SSC CPO CAPF SI Admit Card 2026
SSC CPO Recruitment 2026 has been announced for 1,871 Sub-Inspector (SI) posts in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs). Eligible candidates can apply online from 10/09/2026 to 30/09/2026. The recruitment covers Delhi Police SI (Male & Female), CAPF SI (GD), and CISF SI (Fire) posts. Candidates must fulfill the required educational qualification, age limit, physical and medical standards. The selection process includes Paper-I, PST/PET, Paper-II, Detailed Medical Examination/Review Medical Examination and Document Verification.

NTA UGC NET JRF December 2026 Exam Date Notice
UGC NET December 2026 is conducted for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), Assistant Professor eligibility and Ph.D. admission. The online application process is scheduled from 08/10/2026 to 28/10/2026, while the examination is scheduled from 14/12/2026 to 19/12/2026. Candidates generally require a Master's degree with 55% marks, while OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD/PwBD and Third Gender candidates require 50% marks. Candidates pursuing their final year or awaiting their Master's results may also apply provisionally.

UP Special TET Result 2026
The UPESSC has released the Special Teacher Eligibility Test (Special TET) 2026 under Advertisement No. 03/2026 for teachers working at the Primary and Upper Primary levels and eligible Special Educators (CWSN). The OTR-based online application starts on 05/09/2026, while the examination is scheduled for 03/11/2026. The test will contain 150 MCQs for 150 marks, with a duration of 2 hours 30 minutes and no negative marking. Candidates should carefully review the prescribed eligibility, educational qualification, documents and other requirements before submitting the application.
