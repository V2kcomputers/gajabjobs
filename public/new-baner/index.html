UPESSC PRT Assistant Teacher Online Form 2026
The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has announced the Assistant Teacher Selection Exam 2026 under Advertisement No. 05/2026 for 12,405 vacancies. The recruitment includes 11,508 Assistant Teacher posts in Urban Primary Schools and 897 Assistant Teacher Attached Primary posts in Non-Government Aided Schools. Online applications begin on 16/09/2026, while the written examination will be held on 03 and 04 December 2026. Applicants must meet the prescribed graduation, teacher training and TET requirements according to the respective post. The written examination will have 120 multiple-choice questions for 360 marks, with 1 mark deducted for each incorrect or multiple answer.

UPSSSC Veterinary Pharmacist Eligibility Result 2026
UPSSSC Veterinary Pharmacist Recruitment 2026 has been announced for 1,308 vacancies. Eligible candidates can apply online from 14/09/2026 to 05/10/2026. Applicants must have passed Intermediate with Biology and hold a 2 years 3 months Diploma in Veterinary Pharmacy, along with participation in UPSSSC PET-2025 and a valid PET-2025 Score Card. The selection process includes a main written examination of 100 questions carrying 100 marks, with a 2-hour duration and 1/4 negative marking for each wrong answer.

UPSSSC Livestock Extension Officer LEO Eligibility Result 2026
UPSSSC has announced 1,251 vacancies for Livestock Extension Officer (Pashudhan Prasar Adhikari) under General and Special Selection. Applicants must have appeared in UPSSSC PET 2025 and received a PET 2025 Score Card. The required qualification includes Intermediate (10+2) with Biology and a Two-Year Livestock Extension Diploma from a recognized Veterinary University or College, along with knowledge of Hindi in Devanagari script.

UPSSSC Computer Cadre (Computer Operator) Eligibility Result 2026
UPSSSC Computer Cadre Recruitment 2026 offers 12 vacancies, comprising 11 Computer Operator Grade-A posts and 1 Assistant Computer Operator post. Candidates must hold a graduation degree along with a Diploma in Computer Science or NIELIT/DOEACC ‘O’ Level Certificate and the prescribed experience in Computer Applications. The online application window will remain open from 07/09/2026 to 28/09/2026, with fee payment and correction allowed up to 05/10/2026. The application fee is ₹25 for candidates of all categories. Candidates will be selected through the prescribed main examination, with shortlisting based on the PET-2025 normalized score.
