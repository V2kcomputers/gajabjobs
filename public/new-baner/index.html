SBI Junior Associate Clerk Vacancy 2026
State Bank of India (SBI) has released the Junior Associate (Customer Support & Sales) Special Recruitment Drive 2026 notification to fill 1,538 backlog vacancies for SC, ST, and OBC categories across various States and Union Territories. Eligible candidates can submit their applications online from 07 August 2026 to 27 August 2026. Applicants must possess a Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized university and should be 20 to 28 years of age as on 01 April 2026. The selection process includes a Preliminary Examination, Main Examination, and Local Language Proficiency Test (where applicable). Selected candidates will receive a starting basic pay of ₹26,730, with total monthly emoluments of approximately ₹46,000 in metro cities, along with other allowances and benefits as per SBI rules.
