RRB NTPC Graduate Level Vacancy CEN 06/2026 Online Form
Railway Recruitment Boards have issued an indicative notice for the RRB NTPC Graduate Level Recruitment 2026 under CEN 06/2026. The recruitment covers 3,477 tentative vacancies for various Non-Technical Popular Categories at the graduate level. The vacancies include Goods Train Manager, Chief Commercial-cum-Ticket Supervisor, Junior Accounts Assistant-cum-Typist and Senior Clerk-cum-Typist. Applicants are required to meet the educational and post-specific eligibility conditions prescribed in the detailed CEN. RRB Junior Engineer CEN 04/2026 Revised Exam Date
The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have issued the Indicative Notification (CEN No. 04/2026) for the recruitment of Junior Engineer (JE), Depot Material Superintendent (DMS), and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) posts. A total of 4098 vacancies are available across various Railway Recruitment Boards. The online application process is tentatively scheduled to begin on 14/08/2026 and continue until 13/09/2026. Selected candidates will receive an initial salary of ₹35,400 under Pay Level-6 (7th CPC). Candidates must be between 18 and 33 years of age as on 01/01/2027. The detailed notification will provide post-wise educational qualifications, application fee, selection process, exam pattern, and other recruitment-related information. Applicants are advised to verify and update their Aadhaar details before completing the online application process.

RRB Section Controller CEN 03/2026 Exam City Details 2026
The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have issued the RRB Section Controller Recruitment 2026 short notification under CEN No. 03/2026. A total of 119 vacancies are available for the Section Controller post. Candidates with a Bachelor's Degree from a recognized university can apply for this recruitment. The prescribed age limit is 20 to 33 years as of 01 July 2026. Selected candidates will be appointed under Pay Level-6 and receive an initial salary of ₹35,400 per month. The online application process is tentatively scheduled from 15 July 2026 to 14 August 2026. Applicants are advised to review the detailed notification for complete information regarding eligibility, age relaxation, application fee, and recruitment procedure.
