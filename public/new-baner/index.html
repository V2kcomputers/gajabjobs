AAI Manager & Junior Executive Admit Card 2026
The Airports Authority of India (AAI) has announced AAI Recruitment 2026 under Advertisement No. 12/2026/CHQ/DR-CBT for 389 Manager and Junior Executive posts across multiple disciplines. UIIC Administrative Officer Admit Card 2026
United India Insurance Company Limited (UIIC) has announced the Administrative Officer (Scale-I) Recruitment 2026 for 225 vacancies, comprising 200 Generalist and 25 Hindi Officer posts. UPSSSC PET 2026 Admit Card
The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) has invited online applications for the Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2026. Candidates who have passed Class 10 (High School) or an equivalent examination can apply online from 03 August 2026 to 01 September 2026. Applicants must be between 18 and 40 years of age as on 01 July 2026, with age relaxation available for eligible reserved categories. UPSSSC PET 2026 is a qualifying examination conducted for future Group 'C' recruitment in Uttar Pradesh. The notification includes details regarding eligibility, application fee, important dates, exam pattern, syllabus, and the online application process.
