Indian Air Force Agniveervayu Musician Online Form 2026
Indian Air Force has invited online applications for Agniveervayu (Musician) Intake 01/2027 from unmarried Indian male and female candidates across all States and Union Territories. Eligible candidates who have passed Class 10 (Matriculation) and possess the required musical proficiency with a valid music qualification or experience certificate can apply online from 20 July 2026 to 02 August 2026. The recruitment rally will be conducted from 31 August 2026 to 09 September 2026. The selection process includes document verification, Physical Fitness Test (PFT), English Written Test, Music Proficiency Test, and Medical Examination. Selected candidates will serve under the Agnipath Scheme for four years and will receive salary, allowances, insurance cover, and Seva Nidhi benefits as per Indian Air Force rules.

RRC NFR Apprentice Online Form 2026
Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC), Northeast Frontier Railway (NFR) has invited online applications for 6777 Act Apprentice vacancies under the Apprentices Act, 1961. Candidates are advised to read the official notification before submitting the online application form.
