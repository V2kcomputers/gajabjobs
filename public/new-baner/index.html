UPESSC PRT Assistant Teacher Vacancy 2026
UPESSC has released Assistant Teacher Selection Exam 2026 (Advertisement No. 05/2026) for a total of 12,405 posts, including 11,508 Assistant Teacher posts in Urban Primary Schools and 897 Assistant Teacher Attached Primary posts in Non-Government Aided Schools. The online application process starts on 16/09/2026, with the written examination scheduled for 03 & 04 December 2026. Candidates must fulfill the prescribed educational qualifications, teacher training requirements and TET eligibility as applicable to the post. The written exam will consist of 120 objective-type questions carrying 360 marks, with 1 mark negative marking for each wrong/multiple answer.

UPSC Specialist & Assistant Public Prosecutor Online Form 2026
UPSC has released Advertisement No. 11/2026 for recruitment to 212 posts including Specialist Grade III Assistant Professor, Assistant Editor, Specialist Grade-II, Assistant Public Prosecutor and Public Law Officer/Law Officer. RRB Junior Engineer CEN 04/2026 Exam Date
The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have issued the Indicative Notification (CEN No. 04/2026) for the recruitment of Junior Engineer (JE), Depot Material Superintendent (DMS), and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) posts. A total of 4098 vacancies are available across various Railway Recruitment Boards. The online application process is tentatively scheduled to begin on 14/08/2026 and continue until 13/09/2026. Selected candidates will receive an initial salary of ₹35,400 under Pay Level-6 (7th CPC). Candidates must be between 18 and 33 years of age as on 01/01/2027. The detailed notification will provide post-wise educational qualifications, application fee, selection process, exam pattern, and other recruitment-related information. Applicants are advised to verify and update their Aadhaar details before completing the online application process.

UPESSC Principal Exam City / Admit Card 2026
The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has invited online applications for 111 Principal vacancies in aided degree colleges under Advertisement No. 02/2026. Candidates with a Ph.D. degree, at least 15 years of teaching or research experience, 10 research publications, and the required API score are eligible to apply. The online application process will be open from 21/07/2026 to 20/08/2026, while the written examination is scheduled for 24/09/2026. Selected candidates will be appointed under Pay Level-14 with a salary ranging from ₹1,44,200 to ₹2,18,200 per month. Applicants are advised to verify their eligibility and complete the online application before the closing date.
