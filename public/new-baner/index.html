UPESSC PRT Assistant Teacher Online Form 2026
The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has announced the Assistant Teacher Selection Exam 2026 under Advertisement No. 05/2026 for 12,405 vacancies. The recruitment includes 11,508 Assistant Teacher posts in Urban Primary Schools and 897 Assistant Teacher Attached Primary posts in Non-Government Aided Schools. Online applications begin on 16/09/2026, while the written examination will be held on 03 and 04 December 2026. Applicants must meet the prescribed graduation, teacher training and TET requirements according to the respective post. The written examination will have 120 multiple-choice questions for 360 marks, with 1 mark deducted for each incorrect or multiple answer.

Navodaya Vidyalaya NVS Class 6th Admit Card 2027
Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) has released the JNVST Class 6 Admission 2027 notification for admission into Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas for the academic session 2027–28. Students studying in Class V during the 2026–27 session and meeting the prescribed age criteria can apply online. The selection test is scheduled for 28 November 2026, while the last date to submit the online application is 31 July 2026. Selected candidates will receive free quality education, hostel accommodation, textbooks, uniforms, boarding, and lodging at Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas across India.

MPESB MP Police SI & Subedar Admit Card 2026
MPESB has announced the MP SI / Subedar Recruitment 2026 for 507 posts. Candidates can submit their online applications from 09/09/2026 to 23/09/2026, while the application correction window will remain open until 28/09/2026. The preliminary examination is scheduled to start from 28/10/2026. The recruitment covers Subedar and various Sub-Inspector posts, with educational qualifications varying according to the post. Graduation is required for major posts, while specific technical qualifications are prescribed for technical SI posts. Candidates must meet the prescribed age limit, pay the applicable application fee and fulfill the required PST/PET standards. Candidates should read the official notification carefully for complete eligibility and recruitment conditions.
