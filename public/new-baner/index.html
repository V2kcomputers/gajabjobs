BPSC School Teacher TRE 4.0 Vacancy 2026
Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has released the TRE 4.0 Recruitment 2026 notification for the recruitment of 32,388 School Teachers under the Education Department, Bihar. Candidates should check the prescribed educational qualifications, subject-wise eligibility, age limit and other conditions before applying.

Bank of Baroda Local Bank Officer LBO Online Form 2026
Bank of Baroda (BOB) has invited online applications for 2,482 Local Bank Officer posts under the 2026 recruitment. Eligible candidates should be 21 to 30 years old as on 01/08/2026, have a graduation degree in any discipline and possess at least 1 year of post-qualification experience as an Officer in a Scheduled Commercial Bank. Candidates must also be proficient in the local language of the State they are applying for. The online application process will be open from 18/08/2026 to 07/09/2026.
