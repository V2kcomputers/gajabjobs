UPSC CDS II Admit Card 2026
Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has released the CDS II Recruitment 2026 notification for 451 posts in IMA, INA, AFA and OTA courses. UPSC NDA II Admit Card 2026
Union Public Service Commission has released the UPSC NDA II Recruitment 2026 notification for 394 posts. Interested unmarried male and female candidates can apply online from 20 May 2026 to 09 June 2026. Candidates must have passed 12th class, while Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics are required for Navy and Air Force posts. The NDA II 2026 written exam will be conducted on 13 September 2026 followed by SSB Interview and Medical Examination.

AIIMS NORCETT 11th Nursing Officer Exam City 2026
The All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) has announced the AIIMS NORCET 11 Recruitment 2026 for 2218 Nursing Officer vacancies across AIIMS and participating institutes in India.
