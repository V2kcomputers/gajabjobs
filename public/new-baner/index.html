RRB NTPC Graduate Level Vacancy CEN 06/2026 Online Form
Railway Recruitment Boards have issued an indicative notice for the RRB NTPC Graduate Level Recruitment 2026 under CEN 06/2026. The recruitment covers 3,477 tentative vacancies for various Non-Technical Popular Categories at the graduate level. The vacancies include Goods Train Manager, Chief Commercial-cum-Ticket Supervisor, Junior Accounts Assistant-cum-Typist and Senior Clerk-cum-Typist. Applicants are required to meet the educational and post-specific eligibility conditions prescribed in the detailed CEN. The online application is tentatively scheduled to begin on 08/10/2026 and continue up to 06/11/2026.

UPESSC PRT Assistant Teacher Online Form 2026
The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has announced the Assistant Teacher Selection Exam 2026 under Advertisement No. 05/2026 for 12,405 vacancies. The recruitment includes 11,508 Assistant Teacher posts in Urban Primary Schools and 897 Assistant Teacher Attached Primary posts in Non-Government Aided Schools. Online applications begin on 16/09/2026, while the written examination will be held on 03 and 04 December 2026. Applicants must meet the prescribed graduation, teacher training and TET requirements according to the respective post. The written examination will have 120 multiple-choice questions for 360 marks, with 1 mark deducted for each incorrect or multiple answer.

SSC CGL Tier-I Answer Key 2026
SSC CGL Recruitment 2026 official notification has been released for various Group B and Group C posts across India. Eligible graduate candidates can apply online from 21 May 2026 to 22 June 2026. Candidates can check vacancy details, salary, syllabus, exam date, application fee and complete eligibility before applying online.
