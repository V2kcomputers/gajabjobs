NBEMS Group A, B & C Various Posts Online Form 2026
NBEMS has released the Recruitment 2026 notification for 53 vacancies across various Group B and Group C posts. Indian Overseas Bank Apprentice Online Form 2026
Indian Overseas Bank (IOB) has invited online applications for 750 Apprentice posts for the 2026-27 session. Candidates with a graduation degree from a recognized university can apply online between 07/07/2026 and 20/07/2026. Applicants must be 20 to 28 years of age as on 01/07/2026. The recruitment process consists of an online examination, local language proficiency test, document verification, and medical examination. Selected apprentices will receive a monthly stipend ranging from ₹14,000 to ₹15,500 based on the branch category.

Navodaya Vidyalaya Class 6th Admission 2027-28
Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) has released the JNVST Class 6 Admission 2027 notification for admission into Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas for the academic session 2027–28. Students studying in Class V during the 2026–27 session and meeting the prescribed age criteria can apply online. The selection test is scheduled for 28 November 2026, while the last date to submit the online application is 31 July 2026. Selected candidates will receive free quality education, hostel accommodation, textbooks, uniforms, boarding, and lodging at Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas across India.
