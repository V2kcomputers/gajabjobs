RPSC Physiotherapist Vacancy 2026
The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has released the Physiotherapist Recruitment 2026 notification to fill 20 Physiotherapist (NSA) vacancies under the Medical and Health Department, Government of Rajasthan. The online application process will begin on 23 July 2026 and continue until 21 August 2026. Candidates who have passed Senior Secondary (10+2) and possess a Diploma in Physiotherapy from a recognized institution are eligible to apply. Applicants must also have a working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari Script and knowledge of Rajasthan Culture. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2027, with age relaxation applicable as per Rajasthan Government rules. Interested candidates should read the official notification carefully and submit their applications before the last date.

Delhi High Court Higher Judicial Service HJS Admit Card 2026
The Delhi High Court has invited online applications for the Delhi Higher Judicial Service (DHJS) Recruitment 2026 to fill 27 vacancies through direct recruitment. UPESSC Principal Online Form 2026
The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has invited online applications for 111 Principal vacancies in aided degree colleges under Advertisement No. 02/2026. Candidates with a Ph.D. degree, at least 15 years of teaching or research experience, 10 research publications, and the required API score are eligible to apply. The online application process will be open from 21/07/2026 to 20/08/2026, while the written examination is scheduled for 24/09/2026. Selected candidates will be appointed under Pay Level-14 with a salary ranging from ₹1,44,200 to ₹2,18,200 per month. Applicants are advised to verify their eligibility and complete the online application before the closing date.
