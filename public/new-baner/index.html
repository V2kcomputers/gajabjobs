Army SSC Tech 68th Online Form 2026
The Indian Army has released the SSC (Tech) Recruitment 2027 notification for the 68th Short Service Commission (Technical) Men & Women Courses with a total of 379 vacancies (349 for Men and 30 for Women). Unmarried engineering graduates who meet the prescribed eligibility criteria can apply online through the official website. The online application window for SSC (Tech) Men is from 09/07/2026 to 07/08/2026, while SSC (Tech) Women can apply from 08/07/2026 to 06/08/2026. The selection process includes shortlisting, SSB interview, medical examination, and merit list. Selected candidates will undergo 49 weeks of training at the Officers Training Academy (OTA), Gaya, Bihar, receive a monthly stipend of ₹56,100 during training, and will be commissioned as Lieutenants in the Indian Army upon successful completion of training.

RRC NFR Apprentice Online Form 2026
Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC), Northeast Frontier Railway (NFR) has invited online applications for 6777 Act Apprentice vacancies under the Apprentices Act, 1961. Candidates who have passed Class 10 with a valid NCVT/SCVT ITI certificate in the relevant trade are eligible to apply. For Medical Laboratory Technician trades, candidates must have passed Class 10 and 12th (Science with PCB). The online application window will remain open from 20/07/2026 to 19/08/2026. Selection will be based on the merit of Matriculation and ITI marks, followed by document verification and medical examination. Candidates are advised to read the official notification before submitting the online application form.

UPSSSC OTR Registration 2026 Online Form
UPSSSC has launched the One Time Registration (OTR) 2026 system for candidates applying to future recruitments. Starting from 14 July 2026, applicants must complete their free OTR by submitting personal, educational, and contact details, verifying their mobile number through OTP, and uploading a recent photograph and signature. A unique OTR ID will be generated and can be used for all upcoming UPSSSC recruitment applications.
