NVS Class 11th Admission Online Form 2027-28
Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti has released the notification for Class 11th Lateral Entry Selection Test 2027-28 for admission against vacant seats in Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas. Online applications can be submitted up to 30/09/2026, and the Selection Test will be conducted on 10/04/2027. Candidates must be studying in Class X during the 2026-27 academic session in a Government/Government-recognized school of the district where the JNV is located, and must be Indian Nationals studying Class X in India, with a date of birth between 01/06/2010 and 31/07/2012. The test will consist of 100 objective questions for 100 marks from Mental Ability, English, Science, Social Science and Mathematics, with a duration of 2½ hours and no negative marking. Candidates can opt for a maximum of two streams, subject to available options and vacant seats. The application process is free of cost, and final admission will be subject to document verification, qualifying Class X and availability of seats.

NVS Class 9th Admission Online Form 2027-28
Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti has released the notification for Class 9th Lateral Entry Selection Test 2027-28 for admission against vacant seats in Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas. Online applications can be submitted from 02/09/2026 to 30/09/2026, and the Selection Test will be conducted on 10/04/2027. Candidates must be bona fide residents of the concerned district and must be studying Class VIII during the 2026-27 academic session in a Government/Government-recognized school of the same district where admission is sought. The candidate’s date of birth must be between 01/05/2012 and 31/07/2014. The selection test will consist of 100 objective questions carrying 100 marks from English, Hindi, Mathematics and General Science, with a duration of 2½ hours and no negative marking. The online application is free of cost, and final admission will be subject to document verification and availability of vacant seats.
