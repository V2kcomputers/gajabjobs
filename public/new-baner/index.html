Navodaya Vidyalaya Class 6th Admission 2027
Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) 2027 is conducted by the Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) for admission to Class VI in Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas for the academic session 2027–28. Eligible candidates must be bona fide residents of the concerned district, studying in Class V during the 2026–27 session in a recognized school, and born between 01 May 2015 and 31 July 2017 (both dates inclusive). The online application process is free of cost, and the last date to apply is 31 July 2026. The entrance examination will be held on 28 November 2026, and selected students will receive free education, boarding, lodging, textbooks, and uniforms in the residential schools.

Uttar Pradesh Home Guard DV / PST Admit Card 2026
The Uttar Pradesh Home Guard Recruitment 2025 has announced 41,424 vacancies for Home Guard (Male/Female) posts across all districts of the state. The online application process will run from 18 November 2025 to 17 December 2025. Candidates must have passed Class 10 (High School) from a recognized board and must be between 18 to 30 years of age as on 01 July 2025. The selection process includes Physical Standards Test (PST) and Physical Efficiency Test (PET), where male candidates must complete 4.8 km in 28 minutes and female candidates must complete 2.4 km in 16 minutes. The application fee is ₹400 for General/OBC/EWS and ₹300 for SC/ST candidates. The complete district-wise vacancy list and PET/PST rules are provided in the official notification.
