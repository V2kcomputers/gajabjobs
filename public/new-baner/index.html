Allahabad High Court Research Associate Admit Card 2026
The High Court of Judicature at Allahabad has invited online applications for the post of Research Associate in 2026. Candidates holding or pursuing a 3-year or 5-year LL.B. degree from the academic sessions 2024, 2025, or 2026 are eligible to apply. The recruitment offers a monthly honorarium of ₹40,000 and selection will be based on academic weightage, a Screening Test, and an Interview. RRB Junior Engineer JE Online Form 2026
The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have issued the Indicative Notification (CEN No. 04/2026) for the recruitment of Junior Engineer (JE), Depot Material Superintendent (DMS), and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) posts. A total of 4098 vacancies are available across various Railway Recruitment Boards. The online application process is tentatively scheduled to begin on 14/08/2026 and continue until 13/09/2026. Selected candidates will receive an initial salary of ₹35,400 under Pay Level-6 (7th CPC). Candidates must be between 18 and 33 years of age as on 01/01/2027. The detailed notification will provide post-wise educational qualifications, application fee, selection process, exam pattern, and other recruitment-related information. Applicants are advised to verify and update their Aadhaar details before completing the online application process.
