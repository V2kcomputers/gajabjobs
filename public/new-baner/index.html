Delhi High Court Higher Judicial Service HJS Vacancy 2026
The High Court of Delhi has released the Delhi Higher Judicial Service (DHJS) Recruitment 2026 Notification for 27 vacancies through direct recruitment. Eligible candidates with a Law degree (LLB) and the required judicial or advocacy experience can apply online from 01 July to 15 July 2026. The selection process includes a Preliminary Examination, Mains Examination, and Viva-Voce. Interested candidates should check the eligibility criteria, age limit, application fee, important dates, salary, and other details before submitting the online application form.

RSSB Clerk Grade II & Junior Assistant Admit Card 2026
Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) has released the notification for Clerk Grade-II and Junior Assistant Recruitment 2026. A total of 10,644 posts are available for 12th pass candidates with a valid computer qualification. Online applications will be accepted from 15/01/2026 to 13/02/2026, and the written examination is scheduled on 05/07/2026 and 06/07/2026. The recruitment includes posts in both Non-Scheduled and Scheduled Areas. Eligible candidates can apply online through the SSO Recruitment Portal.

UP Anganwadi Worker Bharti Online Form 2026

RSSB CET Graduate Level Online Form 2026
RSSB has officially released the Rajasthan CET Graduate Level 2026 Notification for candidates seeking graduate-level government jobs in Rajasthan. The online application process will begin on 04 July 2026 and continue until 02 August 2026 through the SSO Portal. Candidates with a Bachelor's Degree from a recognized university are eligible to apply. The CET Graduate Level examination will be held from 01 December to 03 December 2026 and serves as the eligibility test for various posts, including Patwari, Village Development Officer (VDO), Junior Accountant, Platoon Commander, Deputy Jailor, Hostel Superintendent Grade-II, Supervisor, and other graduate-level recruitments notified by RSSB.
