NTA – University Grant Commission (UGC)
आवदेन । तिथिया | आवश्यकता । महत्तपूर्ण लिंक
NTA UGC NET JRF December 2026 Online Form
UGC NET December 2026 is conducted for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), Assistant Professor eligibility and Ph.D. admission. The online application process is scheduled from 08/10/2026 to 28/10/2026, while the examination is scheduled from 14/12/2026 to 19/12/2026. Candidates generally require a Master's degree with 55% marks, while OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD/PwBD and Third Gender candidates require 50% marks. Candidates pursuing their final year or awaiting their Master's results may also apply provisionally.
