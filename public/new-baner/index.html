UP Special TET Online Form 2026
UPESSC has released the Special Teacher Eligibility Test (Special TET) 2026 under Advertisement No. 03/2026 for eligible teachers working at the Primary and Upper Primary levels, along with Special Educators (CWSN). The online application process is based on OTR, with registration starting from 05/09/2026. The examination is scheduled for 03/11/2026. The exam will consist of 150 MCQs carrying 150 marks, with a duration of 2 hours 30 minutes and no negative marking. Candidates should carefully check the prescribed eligibility, qualification, documents and other requirements before applying.

Delhi High Court Personal Assistant PA, SPA Online Form 2026
Delhi High Court has announced the SPA & PA Examination 2026 for 150 posts, including 117 Senior Personal Assistant and 33 Personal Assistant vacancies. Eligible graduates can apply with the required English shorthand and computer typing speed. The age limit is 18–32 years as on 01/01/2026, with applicable relaxation for reserved categories. Online applications will be accepted from 15/09/2026 to 05/10/2026. The selection process includes English Typing Test, English Shorthand Test, Main Descriptive Examination and Interview. Candidates should carefully read the official notification for detailed eligibility, reservation and other recruitment conditions.

PFRDA Assistant Manager Online Form 2026
PFRDA has announced Officer Grade A (Assistant Manager) Recruitment 2026 for 30 vacancies across General, Research, Actuary and Official Language (Rajbhasha) streams. The online application process is scheduled from 03/09/2026 to 24/09/2026. The maximum age limit is 30 years as on 31/07/2026, with applicable relaxations for eligible categories. The application fee is ₹1,000 for GEN/EWS/OBC candidates, while SC/ST/PwBD & Ex-Servicemen candidates are exempted. Selection will consist of Phase-I Online Examination, Phase-II Online Examination and Phase-III Interview.
