BOB Bank of Baroda Wealth Executive Vacancy 2026
Bank of Baroda has released a recruitment notification for 1,100 regular posts in various departments. The vacancies include 1,000 Wealth Executive posts in Wealth Management Services and 100 Credit Analyst–C&IC posts in Corporate & Institutional Credit. The Wealth Executive post requires graduation in any discipline along with one of the prescribed valid certifications such as IC 38 or specified NISM certifications, plus at least 2 years of relevant wealth-product sales experience. The Credit Analyst–C&IC post requires graduation, CA qualification, and at least 1 year of banking experience in a Scheduled Commercial Bank.

UPSSSC Computer Cadre (Computer Operator) Online Form 2026
UPSSSC Computer Cadre Recruitment 2026 offers 12 vacancies, comprising 11 Computer Operator Grade-A posts and 1 Assistant Computer Operator post. Candidates must hold a graduation degree along with a Diploma in Computer Science or NIELIT/DOEACC ‘O’ Level Certificate and the prescribed experience in Computer Applications. The online application window will remain open from 07/09/2026 to 28/09/2026, with fee payment and correction allowed up to 05/10/2026. The application fee is ₹25 for candidates of all categories. Candidates will be selected through the prescribed main examination, with shortlisting based on the PET-2025 normalized score.

UPSC CDS II Admit Card 2026
Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has released the CDS II Recruitment 2026 notification for 451 posts in IMA, INA, AFA and OTA courses. Candidates having Graduation or Engineering Degree can apply online through the UPSC portal from 20 May 2026 to 09 June 2026. The recruitment process includes Written Examination, SSB Interview, Document Verification and Medical Examination. UPSC CDS II 2026 examination will be held on 13 September 2026 across various examination centres in India.
