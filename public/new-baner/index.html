RRB NTPC Inter Level CEN 07/2025 CBT-II Admit Card 2026
The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the notification for RRB NTPC Inter Level Recruitment 2025 (CEN 07/2025) with 3050 vacancies under the 10+2 (Undergraduate) category. Eligible candidates who have passed Intermediate (Class 12th) can apply online for posts like Junior Clerk cum Typist, Accounts Clerk cum Typist, Train Clerk, and Commercial cum Ticket Clerk. The application process will start from 28 October 2025 and continue till 27 November 2025. The pay scale ranges from ₹19,900 to ₹21,700 (Level-2 & Level-3 as per 7th CPC) with additional allowances. Candidates are advised to check the official notification for complete eligibility, age limit, application fees, and exam details.

UPSSSC Veterinary Pharmacist Online Form 2026
UPSSSC Veterinary Pharmacist Recruitment 2026 has been announced for 1,308 vacancies. Eligible candidates can apply online from 14/09/2026 to 05/10/2026. Applicants must have passed Intermediate with Biology and hold a 2 years 3 months Diploma in Veterinary Pharmacy, along with participation in UPSSSC PET-2025 and a valid PET-2025 Score Card. The selection process includes a main written examination of 100 questions carrying 100 marks, with a 2-hour duration and 1/4 negative marking for each wrong answer.
