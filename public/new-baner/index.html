UPESSC PRT Assistant Teacher Online Form 2026
The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has announced the Assistant Teacher Selection Exam 2026 under Advertisement No. 05/2026 for 12,405 vacancies. The recruitment includes 11,508 Assistant Teacher posts in Urban Primary Schools and 897 Assistant Teacher Attached Primary posts in Non-Government Aided Schools. Online applications begin on 16/09/2026, while the written examination will be held on 03 and 04 December 2026. Applicants must meet the prescribed graduation, teacher training and TET requirements according to the respective post. The written examination will have 120 multiple-choice questions for 360 marks, with 1 mark deducted for each incorrect or multiple answer.

UPESSC UP PGT Teacher Online Form 2026
UPESSC has announced the PGT (Lecturer) Selection Examination 2026 through Advertisement No. 06/2026 for 2,607 posts in aided secondary schools of Uttar Pradesh, comprising 2,329 vacancies for boys' schools and 278 vacancies for girls' schools. The OTR and online application process starts from 18/09/2026, the last date for fee payment is 17/10/2026, and application correction is available up to 21/10/2026. The written examination will be conducted on 15/12/2026 and 16/12/2026.

UPSSSC Platoon Commander / Block Organizer Result 2026
UPSSSC has released the Platoon Commander and Block Organizer Recruitment 2026 notification for 295 vacancies. The online application process begins on 16 June 2026 and closes on 06 July 2026. Candidates must hold a graduation degree and qualify PET 2025 to apply. The application fee is ₹25 for all categories. Both male and female candidates are eligible, and the job location will be Uttar Pradesh.

BPSC School Teacher TRE 4.0 Online Form 2026
Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has announced TRE 4.0 Recruitment 2026 for 32,388 School Teacher posts under the Education Department, Bihar. Vacancies include Primary (Classes 1–5), Middle (Classes 6–8), Secondary (Classes 9–10) and Higher Secondary (Classes 11–12) teachers. Online applications will be accepted from 01/09/2026 to 30/09/2026, while the fee payment window is from 01/09/2026 to 29/09/2026. Candidates should carefully check the prescribed qualification, subject-wise eligibility, age limit and other recruitment conditions before applying.
