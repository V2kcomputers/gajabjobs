UPESSC Assistant Professor Admit Card 2026
UPESSC has announced Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 for 1,936 posts in Uttar Pradesh. The online application process through OTR is available from 08/09/2026 to 07/10/2026. The vacancies include 1,564 posts in co-education colleges and 372 posts in women’s colleges. Candidates generally require a Master’s degree in the relevant subject with at least 55% marks, along with NET/SLET/SET or applicable Ph.D. exemption as prescribed in the notification. The written examination will be held on 19/11/2026 and 20/11/2026, followed by an interview. The selection process consists of 360 marks for the written exam and 40 marks for the interview, making a total of 400 marks.

MPESB ASI & Subedar Steno Admit Card 2026
MPESB ASI Steno Recruitment 2026 has been announced for 655 posts of Subedar (Ministerial) Stenographer and Assistant Sub-Inspector (Ministerial) in the Madhya Pradesh Police Department. The online application process will begin on 24/09/2026 and applications can be submitted up to 08/10/2026, with correction facility available until 13/10/2026. The examination is scheduled to commence from 03/11/2026. Applicants generally need to be 12th class pass with the prescribed CPCT/Hindi Typing and computer qualification, while Subedar Stenographer posts also require Hindi shorthand at 100 WPM. The selection process consists of a written examination followed by document verification and practical/skill tests. Candidates should refer to the official notification for detailed eligibility, age limit, reservation, qualification and other applicable requirements before applying.
