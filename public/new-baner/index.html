Patna High Court Assistant Admit Card 2026
Patna High Court has invited online applications for 68 Ex-Cadre Assistant (Group-C) posts under the Assistant Recruitment 2026. Candidates holding a Bachelor's Degree along with a 6-month Computer Application Certificate/Diploma are eligible to apply from 28/07/2026 to 27/08/2026. The recruitment process consists of a Written Examination, Computer Proficiency Test (CPT), and Interview. Selected candidates will be appointed under Pay Level-4 with a salary of ₹25,500–₹81,100. Applicants should complete the online application and fee payment within the prescribed schedule after verifying all eligibility conditions.

MPESB has announced the MP SI / Subedar Recruitment 2026 for 507 posts.
